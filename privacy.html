Privacy Policy

Website privacy policy.

This policy explains what happens when you visit our website (dopplr.digital and related pages): what we collect, the cookies and technologies we use, the partners that help us understand and reach business visitors, and the choices you have. Looking for how we handle shopper data inside the Dopplr product? That's covered separately on our Security & Privacy page.

Effective  August 12, 2026 Last updated  August 12, 2026 Applies to the marketing website
What we collect

Information we collect when you visit.

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us. When you book a call, request a demo, or email us, we collect what you send, such as your name, work email, company, and message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our providers use cookies and similar technologies to record standard data such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring page, and how you interact with the site.
  • Information from data partners. We work with online data partners and vendors that help us recognize business visitors and understand which organizations are engaging with us, as described below.
Cookies & tracking

Cookies and similar technologies.

We use cookies, tags, pixels, and local storage to operate the site, remember preferences, measure traffic, and understand engagement. Some of these technologies are set by third-party providers acting on our behalf. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings; note that parts of the site may not function as intended if you do.

Data partners & visitor identification

How our data partners work, and how to opt out.

To reach the businesses most likely to benefit from Dopplr, we use visitor-identification and marketing technologies (including RB2B) that may associate your website activity with information already held about you, including your business email address, so that we or our providers can follow up. You can opt out at any time using the links below.

Data partner disclosure

When you visit or log in to our website, cookies and similar technologies may be used by our online data partners or vendors to associate these activities with other personal information they or others have about you, including by association with your email. We (or service providers acting on our behalf) may then send communications and marketing to these email addresses.

You may opt out of receiving this advertising and identification by visiting https://app.retention.com/optout.

If you are located in the EU, UK, or EEA, you may also opt out through the GDPR-specific path at https://www.rb2b.com/rb2b-gdpr-opt-out.

How we use it

How we use this information.

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the demos, calls, and information you request.
  • To operate, secure, measure, and improve the website.
  • To identify organizations that may be a fit for Dopplr and send relevant business communications and marketing.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and enforce our terms.
Sharing

Who we share it with.

We share information with service providers and data partners that perform functions on our behalf, such as hosting, analytics, visitor identification, and communications, and only for those purposes. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a corporate transaction. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your choices & rights

Your choices and rights.

  • Opt out of data-partner identification and marketing using the links in the disclosure above.
  • Control cookies through your browser settings.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message.
  • Exercise regional rights. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing. To make a request, contact us at sresht@dopplr.digital.
Retention, transfers & changes

Retention, international transfers, and updates.

Retention. We keep website and contact information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

International transfers. We operate in the United States and India, and our providers may process information in other countries. Where required, we put appropriate safeguards in place for cross-border transfers.

Changes. We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above, and material changes will be reflected on this page.

Note: this policy covers our marketing website only. It is not legal advice; the specific technologies and partners we use may change as we refine our go-to-market.
